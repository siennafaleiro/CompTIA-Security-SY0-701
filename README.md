## Cybersecurity Career Accelerator Repository

This repository is designed to support aspiring cybersecurity professionals on their journey to earning the **[CompTIA Security+ certification](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/security)**—an essential milestone for launching a rewarding career in cybersecurity.

## About CompTIA Security+ Exam

CompTIA Security+ is a globally recognized certification that validates the essential skills needed for entry-level roles in cybersecurity. It covers a comprehensive range of foundational topics, including:

- Cyber threats and vulnerabilities
- Security technologies and tools
- Network security and architecture
- Identity and access management
- Risk management
- Cryptography

## What's Inside?

- **Comprehensive Study Guides**: In-depth guides aligned with each Security+ exam objective, enriched with real-world examples and practical insights to strengthen your understanding.
- **Concise Study Notes**: Easy-to-digest summaries highlighting key concepts, definitions, and exam tips—perfect for quick reviews and last-minute prep.
- **Practice Exams**: Simulated tests designed to mirror the format and difficulty of the actual CompTIA Security+ exam, helping you assess your readiness and identify areas for improvement.

## How to Use This Repository

1. Begin by reviewing the comprehensive study guides to grasp the core concepts and topics covered in the exam.
2. Utilize the concise study notes for quick reference and last-minute revision before the exam.
